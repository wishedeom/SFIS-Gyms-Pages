# FREE-SOURCED — PROTO ONLY

These third-party tracks are prototype material. They are not approved for the final game; the user may approve an individual sourced asset for final use case by case.

Source pack: [Dark Sci-Fi Audio Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-sci-fi-audio-pack), by SRG774. Also listed by the creator at [itch.io](https://srg774.itch.io/dark-sci-fi-audio-pack).

The OpenGameArt and creator pages state CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain. The creator says the music was produced by hand in Ableton Live Suite. The itch.io listing labels the pack AI-assisted for code and text; it does not attribute AI assistance to the music. Attribution is not required, but the source and author are retained here for provenance.

- `Sector_PROTO.mp3`: default hydroponics exploration music.
- `Urgent_PROTO.mp3`: alert-state music, crossfaded in while any living enemy is alerted.

The files were downloaded from the source pack's individual MP3 attachments. No edits or remixes were made. Godot enables MP3 looping at runtime.
