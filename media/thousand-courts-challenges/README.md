# Thousand Courts — challenge increment [PROTO]

Two new optional loops in the inhabited district. Six residents, two public
keepers and one separately authorized store custodian. Existing four
conversations retain their static offline prototype speech.

- **Bonded store:** disputed-roll admission plus public permission, concealed low
  luggage return, actual inside shutter interlock and sight-breaking racks.
  Public permission alone does not pacify the custodian. A real movable crate
  reaches the sorting ledge and Optical Veil core/procedure. One finite knife
  and two rounds supplement existing public supplies.
- **Washhouse works:** move or damage actual cargo, or crouch through the
  independent low side channel. Find one finite baton, a corroborating handling
  tag and an inside return latch into Veil Court. Pressure machinery remains
  isolated; steam/water/gas hazards are not active here.
- **Optional fitting:** the household bench fits recovered Veil with its shared
  costs, cooldown and Z / Augment input. No implant is granted at arrival.

The required route remains completable without a weapon, augmentation,
conversation or consumable. Optional discoveries, fitting, shortcuts and spent
supply receipts persist through replay for this host session; reload starts
fresh. Campaign carryover is not connected. Admission currently covers the
prototype store room; package-specific property/theft reactions are later work.
Receiving-room enemies and negotiations, balcony connections, pressure systems
and rescue remain design proposals.
