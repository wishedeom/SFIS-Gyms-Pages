# Thousand Courts — challenge expansion proposal

Working design, 2026-11-05. The user requested more challenge areas and a joint
consideration of weapons, items, augments and enemies. Names, placements,
quantities, new events and faction roles below are proposals. Target designs are
separate from the playable subset listed below. See
[current implementation and scope](../../Root/Story/ThousandCourts/README.md).
The [challenge topology](THOUSAND_COURTS_CHALLENGE_TOPOLOGY.svg) shows proposed
loops separately from the existing two-deck spatial diagram.

## Current playable subset

The new increment builds two optional loops: the bonded store beneath the archive
and the dry washhouse service annex east of Veil Court. The store has a separate
baton custodian, admission requiring public permission plus the existing disputed
roll, a physically crouched luggage return and an inside shutter interlock.
Public permission alone leaves its custodian hostile. An ordinary movable sorting
crate reaches the 2 m ledge. A knife, two rounds and the Optical Veil core/procedure
are finite/discoverable rewards; a local household bench fits Veil using the
shared capability. The existing public pistol is retained in this increment.

Washhouse cargo can be carried aside or destroyed with the shared damage receiver;
a separate low side channel works without touching it. The service side holds
one baton, a corroborating handling tag and a real return latch into Veil Court.
There is no new washhouse enemy, steam, gas, water exposure or pressure-supply
consequence in this increment. The cargo and crouch approaches are physically
tested; the hazard proposals below remain unbuilt.

Shortcuts, store admission, discoveries, fitted Veil and spent supply receipts
persist through physical replay for this host session. The required service Demo
still completes without visiting these rooms, fitting an augment or spending a
supply. Current optional fitting supplies only Veil; the garden configurations
are not silently granted and campaign carryover remains disconnected.

Store admission currently covers the whole prototype room. Package-specific
property rights, theft reactions and the copyist's release dialogue are later
work. Receiving rooms, their two proposed agents, balcony connections, rescue
and interpreting the new tag in dialogue remain design work. The target diagram
includes those later connections and must not be read as an as-built floorplan.

## Design intent

Develop three connected situations rather than append a series of combat rooms.
Each should expose its problem before committing the player, accept at least
three materially different approaches, and leave a useful connection behind.
Include a safe place to observe or retreat. Difficulty comes from overlapping
constraints, finite resources and consequences, rather than enemy health alone.

The main route must remain completable with ordinary movement, crouching and
interaction, without a weapon, implant, elevated skill grade or expendable item.
An individual optional branch may require a capability and reward a later return.
No single favor or public pass should automatically solve every later situation.

## 1. Washhouse works — approach and machinery

**Original use:** crew laundry, hot-water treatment and utility access.
**Evolved use:** communal washhouse and carefully rationed household water.
**Identity:** indigo screens above pale troughs, low teal pipework, wet service
surfaces and warm occupied rooms overlooking the machinery.

The witness remains reachable in the occupied, safe front room. Beyond it, a
short service crossing is interrupted by a visibly cycling pressure leak. The
player can see the next safe recess and the isolation equipment before entering.
This is an optional way to reach the receiving house's service side and an
optional source of leverage, not a new obstacle on the existing free route.

Approaches:

- Read the cycle and cross between releases using sheltered recesses.
- Approach the isolation valve by a longer dry maintenance loop and stop the
  source. Show which communal machinery also stops; do not silently punish a
  safety action with an unexplained household consequence.
- Move real service cargo into the jet's lane, then cross behind it. Induction
  Grip makes placement possible from cover; ordinary carrying remains viable.
- A separate submerged return can reward Rebreather, provided ordinary swimming
  or the dry loop remains possible. Its Dive Jet can shorten exposure to water;
  respiratory protection does not provide heat protection from steam.

**Reward:** open a service-side latch into Veil Court, recover a transfer handling
tag useful at the receiving house, and optionally restore the washhouse supply.
Keep any resident reward finite and proportionate; this does not award a generic
district-wide pass. No guard is needed here initially: machinery, water and the
effect on occupied rooms supply a different sort of pressure.

## 2. Bonded store — optional equipment under observation

**Original use:** crew luggage and controlled personal-effects storage, beneath
the records floor. **Evolved use:** a keeper-controlled store of confiscated
possessions. **Identity:** chalk cage partitions, numbered racks, clay-red seals;
the archive above becomes an observable ceiling and later route landmark.

The player can inspect a useful weapon and a service case through the grille.
One baton keeper watches the direct aisle. Racks break sightlines and prevent
the second room from being readable from one safe firing position. Give the
keeper a physical patrol with an observable interval, rather than random turns.

Approaches:

- Bring the disputed household roll to the copyist and obtain a narrowly scoped
  release for one recorded package. Consent applies to that package, not the
  entire store. Public passage permission alone does not transfer ownership.
- Enter through a concealed low luggage return and work behind the shelving.
  Reach and release a real rear latch from inside. Ordinary stealth works.
- Move a crate to reach the sorting ledge. Impulse Tendons offers a faster way
  onto the same ledge; Induction Grip can move the crate from a screened position.
- Fight the keeper after preparing; noise and lost permission are real costs.

**Rewards:** relocate the publicly exposed pistol into the store for the later
balance pass, add one baton obtainable without killing, and offer one compatible
core with an Optical Veil procedure. Place the core/procedure in a separate
service case from the released personal property. Do not imply that accepting a
household favor automatically grants the player's implant.

The existing free pistol remains where it is until route and acceptance tests
are deliberately revised. Opening the store should create a connection between
the quiet lower court and the west household route, not a dead-end loot closet.

## 3. Screened House — receiving rooms and divided authority

**Original use:** accessible cabins, a crew reception room and luggage handling.
**Evolved use:** a private transfer address occupied by intermediaries, adjacent
to households that are neither captors nor automatic allies.
**Identity:** indigo privacy screens, packed bedding, old cabin numbers partly
hidden by transfer marks; a bright public reception fronts a screened work room.

The ledger identifies the correct house. A reception threshold allows the player
to observe and talk before entering restricted rooms. Behind it, one pistol agent
watches a long aisle while one knife carrier can move through the side rooms.
Crossfire and flanking make a frontal fight costly; both positions have cover
and escape paths. Start with two opponents, not a wave encounter.

Approaches:

- Present the ledger plus witness testimony or the washhouse handling tag.
  Contradict the claimed authority of the transfer, obtaining an inspection or
  separating the occupants. A household vouch establishes who you are, but is
  not itself proof that their transfer is unlawful.
- Reach the back service entry through the washhouse; open the inner release
  from the quiet side. The ordinary route includes cover and timing.
- Reach the screened balcony from the archive-side walk; Impulse Tendons may
  shorten the climb while a longer stair/ledge route remains available.
- Use light and sound to separate sightlines. Optical Veil can cross one exposed
  section, but still requires care around hearing, close contact and actions.
- Prepare and fight the two agents, preserving a withdrawal route into Veil
  Court. Their finite ammunition is actor-owned and remains finite when looted.

**First playable outcome:** recover the receiving record, release a physical
exit and establish what happened locally. A later rescue must model actual
prisoners, transport and consequences; pressing a console must not pretend to
teleport or rescue unseen sleepers. The record cannot establish the raid's
commissioner or contamination culprit without a separate story decision.

**Return:** the receiving room opens to the public spine and washhouse service
loop. Rooms seen as private barriers on arrival become an understood passage.

## Equipment and threat budget

These are initial playtest budgets for the whole district, not additions to every
existing cache. Count loaded enemy rounds, loose ammunition and carried-in
equipment when balancing. Campaign carryover is not implemented today.

| Equipment | Proposed placement / amount | Purpose and cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary baton | Washhouse service locker or released store package; one | Accessible emergency melee, stamina commitment and dangerous approach distance |
| Service pistol | Bonded store; one, 4–6 player-accessible rounds across the district | Interrupt or resolve a prepared threat; gunfire exposes the player and spends rare ammunition |
| Knife | One discoverable service tool; existing agent fallback | Close utility/emergency weapon; no new cutting capability is assumed |
| Shotgun | Defer normal player placement; possible later optional receiving-house cache | Strong room-scale answer that would otherwise erase this early encounter; requires a distinct ammunition budget |
| Lamp cell | Existing finite household favor supply; do not also require the same unique cell for progress | Meaningful optional expenditure, never a mandatory double spend |
| Field dressing | Two initially, with at most one optional earned replenishment | Existing Medicine ceiling and finite receipt behavior; no full-heal expectation |
| Transfer handling tag | Washhouse service office | Specific evidence, not a reusable master key |
| Disputed household roll | Existing lower court, upgraded to usable evidence | Recontextualize an existing readable as leverage rather than add a duplicate collectible |
| Movable service cargo | One near machinery and one near store ledge | Physical cover, obstruction and climbing; ordinary carry remains useful |

No automatic ammunition replacement after combat. No default enemy death drops
beyond actual owned equipment. No assault rifle, sniper, grenade stockpile or
large creature is needed to make this residential district challenging.

## Augment placement and uses

Assume the player may arrive with none of the garden's optional configurations.
Plan a clear local fitting point, not an automatic grant of all configurations.
Current separate map profiles do not yet provide cross-area carryover.

| Augment | Concrete use here | Baseline alternative | Availability proposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Impulse Tendons | Store sorting ledge; receiving-house balcony | Movable crate or longer stair/ledge loop | Possible garden acquisition; optional local recovery only if carryover remains disconnected |
| Rebreather / gas mask | Washhouse submerged return; respiratory protection where an actual gas source is authored | Dry utility loop; ordinary surface access | Possible garden acquisition; heat immunity is not included |
| Induction Grip | Place a steam-blocking crate or retrieve service cargo from cover | Carry and Drop from the safe side | Possible garden acquisition |
| Optical Veil | One exposed crossing in the receiving work room | Screened side rooms, sound distraction or negotiation | The one proposed new district discovery, in the optional bonded store |
| Echo Lattice, Tension Spool, Resonance Lure | Later revisit opportunities if their actions fit physical geometry | Existing baseline loops remain | Do not distribute all later augments here or assume ownership for first entry |

Optical Veil uses its current stamina/cooldown, hearing, close shimmer and
action-break rules. An optional high cache can remain inaccessible on first visit;
put supplies or supplementary evidence there, not the only receiving address.

## Encounter roles and escalation

- **Household residents:** neutral, with privacy and needs. No generic hostile
  population and no garden-cannibal reuse.
- **Passage keepers:** baton pressure at doors and a finite-pistol watch on the
  archive stair. Public authorization covers these public duties.
- **Store custodian:** one baton keeper; specific release and trespass rules.
  Avoid a third generic armed checkpoint on the same mandatory route.
- **Transfer agents:** one limited-pistol sentry plus one mobile knife carrier
  inside the receiving house. Public authorization does not pacify them.
- **Ecology:** reserve insects or plant intrusions for one plausible neglected
  service pocket later. No meat beast or respawning insect swarm is needed here.

Keep encounter groups spatially separated, so alert does not summon the whole
map through neutral homes. Alert propagation and responses to civilian assault
must be implemented before claims of district-wide social consequences.

## Build sequence and acceptance

1. Build the bonded-store loop and physical rear latch, one bounded encounter,
   finite equipment and optional ledge reward. Preserve all current baseline
   routes. Validate actual movement, Use, receipt persistence and enemy ownership.
2. Build the washhouse utility loop using shared hazards/cargo/water. Validate
   source warning, damage to appropriate actors, real counterplay, no double dose,
   isolation cost and reset. New shared hazard work also belongs in Hazard works.
3. Build receiving-room approaches and the local outcome; add evidence-specific
   dialogue and separate hostile authority. Verify that public permission cannot
   bypass private encounters, and that optional evidence is not mandatory.
4. Place the one new augment discovery and fitting point; validate both augmented
   and baseline routes. Rebalance the public pistol only when its new route works.

Use test traversal through actual geometry, not teleportation, to prove each
baseline bypass. Check replay, resource exhaustion, retreat and back shortcuts.
After population is final, profile the active district in browser and preserve
mobile controls. Publish playable increments with current limitations stated.
