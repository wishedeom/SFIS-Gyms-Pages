    WHAT TO WATCH
    A goal, not a route.
    The autonomous player searches for extraction without knowing its location. It reacts to visible guards, uncertain footstep directions, and rising alertness. Cyan rings are player footsteps; red rings are guards.
    LATEST HEARING No observation yet
    Make it your attempt.
    Select Control or Take control. On a computer, click the game for mouse look: WASD moves, Space jumps, C crouches, Shift sprints, Q/E lean, and F interacts with nearby covers, crates, pickups and objectives. Escape releases the mouse.
    On a phone, use the lower-left stick to move and hold the right look stick off-centre to turn; turn speed follows its deflection. The action pad sits above the look stick. Tap Sprint or Crouch to toggle them. Hold Lean L/R to peek; release to centre. Jump and Sprint exit crouch. Tap Use for covers, pickups and crates; Use again drops a held crate. Full screen is at the top right of the game; tap it again to exit. These are basic development controls.
    Replays use the loaded files. Later visits can reuse the cached engine.
